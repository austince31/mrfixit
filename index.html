FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you really not upsell repairs I don't need?
Correct. Neighbors regularly tell me other shops quoted a long list of repairs, and I found only one real issue. If your car doesn't need it, I'll tell you — even if that means a smaller bill.
Can you come to me instead of towing my car in?
Often, yes. I've driven out for stranded neighbors, including on days the shop was closed. Call and describe the situation and I'll let you know if an on-site repair makes sense.
What kind of repairs do you handle?
Everything from oil changes and brakes to transmissions, electrical work, A/C, steering and suspension, and full engine replacement. If it's under the hood, it's likely something we handle.
How fast can you get my car back to me?
Most common repairs are turned around in a day or two. I'll give you a straight timeline up front, and I'll let you know if anything changes.
Do you charge for small fixes or a quick look?
Not always. For a quick, minor fix, I've waived the charge rather than bill for a few minutes of work.
Are you open on weekends?
The shop is open Monday through Friday, 9am to 5:30pm. For a true emergency outside those hours, call anyway — I've made exceptions for neighbors in a bind.
